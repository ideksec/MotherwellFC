## Results

### Motherwell's form — three unbeaten, and the xG has turned before the results have

Motherwell go into the break-ending fixture 9th of 12 on 13 points from 13 league matches, 3-4-6 with 15 scored and 21 conceded. The last five read LLDWD for 5 points, 6 goals for and 8 against, but the underlying numbers are kinder: across the four of those matches with xG, Motherwell created 6.83 and allowed 4.61, a difference of +2.22 and 6.91 expected points against the 5 actually banked. Over the season the gap is smaller and still in Motherwell's favour, 17.40 xG for against 18.54 conceded across the 12 matches with xG, and 16.97 expected points against 13.

Fir Park has not been a fortress. The home record is 2-1-3 (DWLLLW) for 7 points, with 4 goals scored and 8 conceded, and three straight home defeats to Aberdeen, Celtic and Hearts before the 1-0 win over St Mirren on 31 October. What the home matches do show is shot suppression: Motherwell have faced 11.7 shots per match at Fir Park against 19.3 away, and conceded 4.0 corners per home match against 7.4 on the road.

The goals have been spread thin. Lukas Fadinger and Alexander Lowry lead with 3 league goals each, and all three of Fadinger's are penalties, Motherwell's only three league penalties of the season. Ibrahim Sa'Id, Tawanda Maswanhise and Seb Palmer-Houlden have 2 apiece. Tom Sparrow and Maswanhise have 2 assists each. Over the last five matches Palmer-Houlden and Sa'Id lead the packs' top-three shot lists with 11 each, and Sa'Id has 6 of those on target, the most of anyone. The shape has been a 4-2-3-1 in all five, with Alex Paulsen, Martin Moormann, Emmanuel Longelo, Sparrow, Joseph Hodge, Palmer-Houlden, Sa'Id and Maswanhise starting every one and Jake Girdwood-Reich four.

### The opposition — second, one defeat in twelve, and a habit of winning by a goal

The table has Rangers 2nd on 27 points from 12 matches, 8-3-1 with 21 scored and 9 conceded, four behind Celtic who have played a match more. The committed SPFL results file runs only to 20 September, so it covers Rangers' first seven matches: 5-1-1, 8 goals for and 4 against, with 14.22 xG for and 7.02 against. The one defeat was 1-2 at home to Hibernian on 9 August. Away from Ibrox in that file they drew 1-1 at Dundee United and then won 1-0 at Aberdeen, 2-1 at Falkirk and 1-0 at Celtic Park, scoring 5 and conceding 2 on 6.10 xG for and 3.66 against. Every one of the seven results was decided by a single goal or level, and Rangers scored exactly once in six of them.

Last season Rangers finished third on 72 points, 20-12-6 with 76 scored and 43 conceded, behind Celtic on 82 and Hearts on 80; Motherwell were fourth on 61.

The head-to-head has shifted. The last five meetings are D 1-1 (Fir Park, August 2025), L 0-1 (Ibrox, December 2025), D 1-1 (Fir Park, February 2026), W 3-2 (Ibrox, April 2026) and L 0-1 (Ibrox, 5 September this season). Motherwell have not beaten Rangers at Fir Park in the history file, which goes back to 2021-22, but the last three home meetings have all been drawn, after four straight home defeats before that. At Ibrox in September Ryan Naderi scored in the 12th minute and Kim Min-su missed a penalty in the 30th; Motherwell had 59% of the ball but were outshot 27 to 8 and out-created 3.93 to 0.51 on xG, with no card shown to either side. The market priced Motherwell at 7.00 that day, a 13.2% win probability.

### What to watch — shot volume, the penalty spot, and the bookings column

**Can Fir Park keep the shot count down?** The two Old Firm sides Motherwell have faced this season each piled up attempts: Rangers' 27 at Ibrox, and Celtic's 25 to Motherwell's 8 at Fir Park on 11 October, when the xG was 2.85 to 1.05 and possession 34%. Motherwell's home average of 11.7 shots conceded is built against the rest of the league. Holding Rangers nearer that figure than the 25 to 27 of the Old Firm matches would be the clearest sign of a good night.

**Set pieces and penalties.** Rangers have averaged 7.4 corners for and 4.7 against per match in the SPFL file; Motherwell's season corner count is 71 for and 76 against, and they concede 4.0 per home match. At the other end, Fadinger has scored all three of his penalties and two came in the last three matches, while Rangers missed theirs at Ibrox. In a fixture where the file says a single goal usually settles it, one spot kick either way could be the match.

**Discipline.** Motherwell have 23 yellow cards and no reds in 13 league matches; Sparrow and Moormann have 4 each and are the ones to watch for a second-half caution. The September meeting produced no cards at all.

**Team news.** Per the club's pre-match briefing, Alfred Johansson [previewed the match on 19 November](https://www.motherwellfc.co.uk/2026/11/19/alfred-johansson-previews-rangers-2/). For Rangers, reports say [Youssef Chermiti, Lawrence Shankland, Cammy Devlin and Daisuke Yokota are unlikely to feature](https://www.livescore.com/en/news/football/team/rangers/daily/saturday-rangers-daily-news-20261121/), with [Ross McCrorie back in contention and Kim Min-su a doubt](https://www.scotsman.com/sport/football/rangers/latest-rangers-news/motherwell-vs-rangers-injury-news-6-out-and-several-doubts-as-derek-mcinnes-waits-on-two-at-fir-park-9356865), and that Derek McInnes [reported no new injuries from the international break](https://www.rangersreview.co.uk/interviews/26738970.mcinnes-delivers-rangers-injury-update-break/). ## Limitations

Five-match samples; one of the last five (Kilmarnock) has no xG. Rangers' committed results file stops on 20 September, so their last five league matches are summarised only by the table. No pre-match market price for tonight is in the files. Lineups, injuries and suspensions come from web reports, not from committed data, and may change before kick-off.

## Takeaway

Motherwell arrive unbeaten in three with a last-five xG difference of +2.22, but they have lost three of six at Fir Park and have never beaten Rangers there in the five seasons on file, with the last three meetings here all drawn. Rangers win narrowly and often: all seven of their results in the file were by a goal or level, and they outshot Motherwell 27 to 8 in September. A good night looks like a shot count nearer Motherwell's home average of 11.7 than the 25 to 27 the Old Firm sides have posted, Sa'Id and Palmer-Houlden getting their usual volume at the other end, and a fourth consecutive home result against Rangers that is not a defeat.
